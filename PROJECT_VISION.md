# NFL Edge Lab — Project Vision

Build a rigorous, transparent, continuously improving NFL forecasting and betting research system over
multiple seasons (2026–2029).

The system answers, for every game and at an explicit information cutoff:

1. Given everything knowable at that moment, what should the fair spread and fair moneyline have been?
2. How different was that fair price from the actual betting market?
3. Was the difference large enough to justify a wager?
4. Did the model consistently identify profitable opportunities over time?

Principles that do not change:

- Capture broadly. Model selectively. Test rigorously. Preserve history. Improve continuously.
- Every variable is a hypothesis until it survives out-of-sample testing.
- Never use information that was not available at prediction time.
- Never fabricate missing information.
- Prefer robust simplicity over impressive complexity; the simple baseline and the market are the benchmarks.
- No agent is the sole judge of its own work; important work passes independent audit.
- Preserve disagreements and failures instead of hiding them.
- The model ledger and the personal ledger stay separate so model, market and human can each be scored.

Success is judged over seasons by data quality, calibration, closing-line value, out-of-sample
robustness, reproducibility and audit quality — not by one week's record.

The objective is not an impressive AI system. It is a research system whose conclusions become
increasingly trustworthy because they survive historical testing, out-of-sample testing, live
performance, market comparison, reproducibility checks, adversarial auditing and multiple seasons of
evidence.
